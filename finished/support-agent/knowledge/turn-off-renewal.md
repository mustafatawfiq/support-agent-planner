# Turn off renewal (cancel)

Last checked: 2026-10-01 · Owner: Billing · Link: https://kitebridge.example/help/cancel

1. Log in and open Account, then Billing.
2. Click "Turn off renewal" and confirm.
3. You keep access until the end of the time you paid for. You are not charged again.

You can turn renewal back on any time before your access ends.
