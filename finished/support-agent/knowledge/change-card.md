# Change the card on file

Last checked: 2026-10-01 · Owner: Billing · Link: https://kitebridge.example/help/change-card

1. Log in and open Account, then Billing.
2. Click "Payment method", then "Replace card".
3. Enter the new card and click Save. Your next payment uses the new card.

Change the card at least one day before your next payment date.
