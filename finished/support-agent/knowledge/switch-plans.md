# Switch to Pro, or change plans

Last checked: 2026-10-01 · Owner: Support lead · Link: https://kitebridge.example/help/switch

1. Log in and open Account, then Plan.
2. Click "Switch to Pro" and pick monthly or yearly.
3. The page shows the exact amount before you pay. When you move up, you pay only the difference
   for the days left in your current period. Your next payment is the new price.

Moving down to Starter takes effect at your next payment date.
