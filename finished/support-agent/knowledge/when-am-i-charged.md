# When am I charged?

Last checked: 2026-10-01 · Owner: Billing · Link: https://kitebridge.example/help/payments

- Monthly plans are charged on the same day each month as the day you joined.
- Yearly plans are charged once a year, on the day you joined.
- You can see your next payment date and amount under Account, then Billing.
- Prices are in US dollars. Your bank may add a fee for other currencies.
