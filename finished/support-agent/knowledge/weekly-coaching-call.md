# Weekly coaching call (Pro)

Last checked: 2026-10-01 · Owner: Community lead · Link: https://kitebridge.example/help/coaching-call

- The group coaching call is every Wednesday at 17:00 UTC and lasts one hour.
- The join link is in the members area under Calendar. It is the same link every week.
- Recordings are in the members area under Calls, within 24 hours of the call.
- The call is part of Pro plans. Starter members get one live Q&A call a month instead.
