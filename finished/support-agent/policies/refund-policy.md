# Refund policy

Version: 2026-10-13 · Owner: Support lead · Link: https://kitebridge.example/refunds
This policy is made up for the demo.

## The rules

The refund tool reads the numbers in this table. Change a number here and the rule changes.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Refund window, days after the charge | 14 |
| Refunds per member in 12 months | 1 |
| Days until the money is back on the card | 5 to 10 working days |

## In plain words

1. You can get a refund for a charge within 14 days of that charge. This is the same for monthly
   and yearly plans.
2. One refund per member in any 12 months.
3. After 14 days we do not refund the charge. You can turn off renewal so you are not charged again.
   You keep access until the end of the time you paid for.
4. When a refund is approved, your membership ends that day. The money is back on your card in
   5 to 10 working days.
5. If our support agent says a charge is not eligible, you can ask a person to review it at
   https://kitebridge.example/review. A person answers every review request within 2 working days.
